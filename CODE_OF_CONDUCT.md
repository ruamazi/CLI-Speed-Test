# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful and harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
level of contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and discuss ideas rather than people.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other communication made on behalf of the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the
project maintainers. You can find contact options on the maintainers' GitHub
profiles. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

For platform-level abuse, use GitHub's
[report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) form.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports in good faith and may take any action they
consider appropriate, including warnings, moderation, or removal from project
spaces. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This code of conduct is a lightweight project policy, not legal advice.
